# Fever

Fever is a body temperature that is higher than normal. A normal body temperature varies from person to person, but it is generally around 98.6°F (37°C). A temperature above 100.4°F (38°C) most often means that a person has a fever.

## What causes fever?

Fever is usually a sign that the body is responding to an illness or infection. Infections are a common cause of fever. Fever can occur with many different illnesses and does not itself identify a specific disease.

## Symptoms associated with fever

Fever may occur along with other symptoms depending on its cause. Common symptoms that can occur with fever include chills, feeling cold, sweating, headache, muscle aches, fatigue, and decreased appetite.

## Fever and medical evaluation

The significance of a fever depends on factors such as the person's age, temperature, duration of the fever, and other symptoms. A persistent or high fever, or a fever accompanied by concerning symptoms, may require medical evaluation.

## General information

Fever is part of the body's response to illness or infection. It is a symptom rather than a disease itself. The underlying cause of a fever determines what other symptoms may occur and whether medical evaluation is needed.
